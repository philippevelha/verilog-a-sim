An Open-Source Electronic-Photonic Co-Design Flow for Microwave Photonics Based on Verilog-A Envelope Models

1. Introduction

Gap: RF flows can’t represent optical carriers; photonic simulators are proprietary and weak on RF metrics.
Contributions: (i) envelope-based Verilog-A library, (ii) open toolchain, (iii) validation against analytic, published, and commercial references, (iv) co-design case studies.

2. Background and related work

MWP links and metrics (gain, NF, SFDR, IIP3)
Photonic compact modelling and PDK Verilog-A efforts
Open simulators: verilog-a-sim (our)
Position: what is MWP-specific versus generic photonic compact modelling

3. Modelling formalism

Envelope + carrier-tag representation, optical discipline, port conventions
WDM channel handling and polarization scope (explicitly bounded)
Dispersion via rational approximation (vector fitting), with the bandwidth limit quantified
Noise: shot, RIN, thermal, ASE; field vs. intensity noise
Validity region: where the envelope approximation breaks down

4. Component library

Sources: laser (RIN, linewidth)
Modulators: MZM, PM, IQ
Passives: ring/racetrack, coupler/MMI, delay line, fibre
Detectors: PD, balanced PD, with saturation and bandwidth
Optional: SOA
Parameter extraction and calibration from measured data

5. Toolchain architecture

Netlist flow: electrical driver, photonic circuit, and receiver in one schematic/netlist
Simulator backends and portability (Verilog-A compliance across ngspice/OpenVAF, Xyce, yours)
Analyses: DC, transient, harmonic balance/AC, noise; where each backend is limited
Automation: Python layer for sweeps, metric extraction (SFDR, NF), and regression tests
Reproducibility: containerised flow, CI, Apache licence

6. Validation

Analytic: MZM at quadrature (gain, IP3), ring transfer function, IMDD link closed forms
Cross-simulator consistency (same netlist, different backends)
Published measurements and/or commercial tool comparison
Convergence, runtime, and scaling (high-Q rings, long delays, number of tones)

7. Co-design case studies

IMDD link with driver and TIA: bias and Vπ trade-offs
Ring-based MWP filter (notch/bandpass) with electronic tuning loop
Opto-electronic oscillator: loop gain, delay, nonlinearity, phase noise
Photonic-assisted downconverter

8. Discussion

Limits: dispersion bandwidth, port-count growth, stiffness
Extensions: thermal and mechanical coupling (link to your multiphysics benchmarks), PDK integration
