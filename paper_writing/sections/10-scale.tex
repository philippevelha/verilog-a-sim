\section{Transistor-Level ISCAS'85}
\label{sec:scale}

The ISCAS'85 combinational benchmarks~\cite{brglez1985iscas} were turned into transistor-level
decks: every gate a subcircuit of PSP103~\cite{gildenblat2006psp} devices from a published
c7552 cell library, every net an RC subcircuit, $V_{DD}=\SI{1.8}{V}$. Gates the library lacks
are composed from its cells, so the logic is exact but the timing is not a single cell's. Each
netlist was cross-checked gate for gate against an independent copy on \num{4096} random
vectors. \todo{Cite the c7552 deck's source (spicegen.pl, UIC VLSI group) and the PSP103 card
provenance.}
